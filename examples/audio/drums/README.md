# Recorded drum one-shots

These WAV files are from **Pearl Master Studio Pack 1 by enoe**:
https://oramics.github.io/sampled/DRUMS/pearl-master-studio/

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The bridge example uses the recorded kick, three snares, closed/open hi-hat,
ride, crash, and three toms to build its 120 BPM breakbeat. The original WAVs
are included here. `build_soundtrack.py` shortens and fades their tails, varies
their placement and level, and mixes them with original bass and harmony.
